A panel of child welfare professionals discuss their success partnering with schools to foster safe and effective alternatives to connecting families with services.

Panelists included:

* Donna Brailsford, New York City Public Schools
* Katherine Haver, Administration for Children's Services, New York City
* Michelle Lefebvre, Hennepin County, MN
* Sarah Glass, San Diego County, Child and Family Well-Being Department
* Tanina Seagraves, Franklin County Children Services (Ohio)

The prevention collaborative is supported by the Doris Duke Foundation as part of the [OPT-In for Families Initiative](https://www.ddf-opt-in.org/).

A panel of child welfare professionals discuss different approaches to hosting warmlines—building their own 211, embedding within an existing 211, embedding within an existing child welfare hotline, or operating outside of child welfare entirely.

Panelists included:

* Jeffery Hanna, Division Chief, Child Protection Hotline, Los Angeles
* Sarah Hanselin, Department of Human Services 211 Liaison, Colorado
* Sharafdeen Ibraheem, Deputy Director, Office of Thriving Families, Washington DC
* Team members from Safe & Sound, San Francisco

The prevention collaborative is supported by the Doris Duke Foundation as part of the [OPT-In for Families Initiative](https://www.ddf-opt-in.org/).

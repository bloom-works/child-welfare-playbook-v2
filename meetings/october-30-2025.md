The Bloom Works team and San Diego County shared their recently completed research in San Diego County that explored the experiences of families navigating social services. 

During this project, researchers spoke with:

* Service providers and local community experts to understand the existing support ecosystem
* Families to understand their lived experiences trying to navigate that ecosystem to get the support they need

In this session they share insights and recommendations from this work, leaving space for discussion of how these learnings may apply beyond San Diego County.

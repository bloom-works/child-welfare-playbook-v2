Definitions of "relative" vary between states, with many excluding certain kinds of kin caregivers. Blood relatives and specific legal family members may qualify, but other people with deep, meaningful connections may not. As a result, youth may be placed with foster families instead of godparents, family friends, and trusted community members they already know.

As states implement kin licensing standards, their definition of "relative" is particularly important. Broad definitions keep youth connected to their communities and supportive relationships.

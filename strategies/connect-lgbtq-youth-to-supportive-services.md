LGBTQ+ youth need access to gender-affirming healthcare, supportive schools, mentors, and community organizations, but caseworkers often don't know how to connect them to these resources. Identify providers and organizations in your area that can support these youth so staff can make referrals.

## How to do this

**Identify healthcare providers who specialize in LGBTQ+ care.** This includes medical providers and mental health counselors with expertise in gender-affirming care and services for LGBTQ+ youth. Look for directories of lgbtq-competent providers and networks of affirming therapists.

**Identify schools with strong LGBTQ+ support systems.** Learn which schools in your area have trained staff, support programs for LGBTQ+ students, and protective policies. Use this information when making placement decisions and best interest determinations.

**Identify LGBTQ+ community organizations.** Find organizations that provide support groups, community connections, and resources for LGBTQ+ youth. This includes both national organizations with local chapters and community-based groups in your area.

**Develop a process for connecting youth to gender-affirming healthcare.** Create procedures that caseworkers can follow for making referrals and supporting youth in accessing services.

**Connect youth to peer support and community programs.** Help youth access programs where they can meet other LGBTQ+ youth and build community connections.

**Connect youth with LGBTQ+ role models and mentors.** Identify programs where youth can connect with supportive LGBTQ+ adults.

**Identify programs that integrate faith and LGBTQ+ identity.** Some youth and families want support that affirms both their religious faith and LGBTQ+ identities. Find resources that help families navigate this intersection.

## This strategy in action

**Bloom Works** research found that access to gender-affirming medical and mental health providers, peer support programs, and community connections are essential to fully meeting the needs of LGBTQ+ youth in foster care.

Over the past few years, Bloom Works has conducted original research into some of child welfare's most persistent challenges. That research is now available as 19 new strategies and 7 updated strategies in the Child Welfare Playbook.

The new and updated content covers five topic areas: 

* Prevention
* Kin engagement
* Foster parent recruitment
* Caregiver retention
* Supporting LGBTQ+ youth

## New strategies

* Make LGBTQ+ affirmation an agency-wide priority
* Build capacity to support LGBTQ+ youth
* Collect data on sexual orientation and gender identity
* Recruit and develop foster caregivers to support LGBTQ+ youth
* Connect LGBTQ+ youth to supportive services
* Screen caregivers for willingness to grow
* Help foster caregivers develop skills to support a wider range of youth needs
* Share complete information during placement conversations
* Create peer support networks for caregivers
* Reduce caseloads so staff have more time to support youth and caregivers
* Start with small ways to get potential caregivers involved
* Distinguish treatment foster care clearly during recruitment
* Provide specialized support before crisis
* Track what recruitment methods work
* Keep prospective caregivers engaged over time
* Help kin access legal support
* Provide equal support for all permanency options
* Provide emotional support when placing youth with kin
* Assign navigators to support kin throughout their journey

## Updated strategies

* Improve kin communications through user feedback
* Create new payment models for caregivers
* Provide convenient training for active caregivers
* Document and track kin connections
* Assign someone dedicated to engaging kin
* Evaluate referral sources for bias
* Help kin caregivers access financial assistance
